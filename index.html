BhumiSync Application Tutorial & Guide

NESFIC-D-49 · Revenue & Disaster Management, Assam

1 What Does This Application Do?

BhumiSync (ভূমি-সংযোজক) solves the core challenge of NESFIC-D-49: "One Change, Everywhere: Automatic Synchronisation Across Land Record Systems in Assam". In Assam, textual records (Dharitree), cadastral maps (Bhunaksha), and deed registrations (NGDRS) reside in disconnected silos. BhumiSync is the real-time event-driven middleware connecting all three so that a deed registration or mutation instantly and atomically propagates across all platforms in under 2 seconds.

2 How to Use the Live Prototype Simulator

  • Click "Simulate Valid Mutation": Triggers a deed execution event for Dag #588 (2.50 Bighas). You will see the pipeline progress through CDC ingestion, distributed Dag locking, PostGIS Euler-Poincaré geometric verification, and atomic commit. The Jamabandi table and Cadastral vector map split synchronously into Dag 588/1 (1.50 B) and Dag 588/2 (1.00 B) in ~420 ms.
  • Click "Simulate Boundary Overlap (Chaos Rollback)": Simulates an erroneous or conflicting land partition. The GeoSync engine detects the polygon overlap, halts the workflow, and triggers an automated Saga compensating rollback—demonstrating 100% data integrity without database corruption.

3 Key Architectural Guarantees

Distributed Redlock: Locks Dags at SRO stage to eliminate double registration fraud.
Euler Invariant: Validates planarity & area conservation (Δ = 0.00 sq m).
